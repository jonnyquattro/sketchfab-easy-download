Adaptation: build.mjs injects a scoped fetch adapter into sf-api.js so anonymous
Tampermonkey requests can be used without a local helper. The embedded WASM
bytes and original source are retained. Geometry-only export omits textures,
animations and wireframe payloads. The separately written print exporters apply
scene transforms, support optional user-chosen scale, and convert to Z-up.
